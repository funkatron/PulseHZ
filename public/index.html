Capture & BPM tips

Pick a loopback device (e.g. BlackHole on macOS) to tap another app’s output, or your mic. Browsers list loopback as an audio input. Use headphones if you monitor a live mic.

With live capture on, tempo is estimated continuously from the input (fast). Use Detect BPM for a one-shot from a file, or a longer refinement while live.

Export notes & demo LFO

ProRes: black/white use an opaque YUV 4:4:4 plate. With Transparent backdrop, export uses ProRes 4444 with alpha (yuva444p10le): a luma + chroma + alpha plane. Clips with alpha or translucent comp pixels carry through where filters preserve alpha; many players flatten alpha when previewing.

Adds a BPM-locked sine route in controls.modulation; uses transport time. ProRes export still ignores automation until baked into the FFmpeg graph.
